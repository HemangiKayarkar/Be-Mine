# Happy Boyfriend's Day ❤️ — Private Love Letter Website

A bespoke, intimate, mobile-first website created as a digital love letter and a journey through your relationship.

---

## 🌟 Features Included

- **Landing Section:** Full-screen romantic opening with *"To my favourite person ❤️"* and cinematic *"Open this ❤️"* interaction.
- **Photo Intro:** Prominent editorial polaroid featuring your favourite photo and heartfelt caption.
- **"Us, in little moments" (Story Timeline):** Vertical mobile timeline with glowing nodes connecting chapters ("The beginning...", "That day...", "One of my favourite memories...", "A moment I'll always keep...", "Us ❤️").
- **"Some of my favourite versions of us" (Memories Gallery):** Horizontal swipeable polaroid photo gallery with subtle vintage tilt angles and tap-to-enlarge full-screen lightbox.
- **"Little things I love about you":** Interactive cards that reveal personal heartfelt notes upon tapping.
- **"Something I want you to know..." (A Letter For You):** Romantic handwritten-style stationery with wax seal aesthetic.
- **"Just you + me" (Our Little Universe):** Dreamy twilight atmosphere with floating starry particles and custom inside memories & facts.
- **"One last thing..." (Surprise Section):** Secret confession revealed with a sparkle burst and a hidden photo.
- **Final Section:** Beautiful farewell photo, emotional signoff (*"Thank you for being you"*), and *"Replay our story"* smooth scroll button.
- **"♫ Our song" Audio Player:** Elegant floating music toggle (no loud autoplay, with built-in romantic acoustic piano fallback if no MP3 is added yet!).
- **Photo Customizer Drawer:** Tap the "Photos" pill in the header to select photos directly from your phone/computer for instant live preview without touching code.

---

## 📸 How to Add Your Photos

You can add your photos in either of two ways:

### Method 1: Drop Files into the `images/` Folder
Place your photos in the `images/` folder with these filenames:
- `images/intro.jpg`
- `images/story-1.jpg`, `images/story-2.jpg`, `images/story-3.jpg`, `images/story-4.jpg`, `images/story-5.jpg`
- `images/memory-1.jpg` through `images/memory-6.jpg`
- `images/surprise.jpg`
- `images/final.jpg`

### Method 2: Instant Browser Upload
Open `index.html` in your browser and click on any photo card or tap the **Photos** button in the top navigation to select photos right from your device.

---

## ✍️ How to Edit Texts & Memories

Open `config.js` in any text editor (like Notepad or VS Code). Every section has clear labels and comments:
- Update your story captions and titles
- Customize the *"Little things I love about you"* cards
- Personalize the inside jokes in *"Our Little Universe"*
- Edit the handwritten letter

---

## 🎵 How to Add Your Song

1. Place your MP3 audio file into the `audio/` folder.
2. Name it `our-song.mp3`.
3. When your boyfriend taps **"♫ Our song"**, it will play!

---

## 📱 How to Run & Share

1. **To test locally:** Double-click `index.html` to open it in Chrome, Safari, or Edge.
2. **To share with your boyfriend:**
   - You can upload this folder to free hosting services like [Netlify Drop](https://app.netlify.com/drop), [Vercel](https://vercel.com), or [GitHub Pages] in 30 seconds to get a private link you can text him!
