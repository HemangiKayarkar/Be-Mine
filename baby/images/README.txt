====================================================================
  HOW TO ADD YOUR PERSONAL PHOTOS
====================================================================

You can add your real photos in TWO ways:

--- OPTION 1: DROP YOUR PHOTOS IN THIS "images" FOLDER ---
Simply copy your photos into this folder with these names:

  • intro.jpg       - Your most beautiful photo together (at the top)
  • story-1.jpg     - "The beginning..."
  • story-2.jpg     - "That day..."
  • story-3.jpg     - "One of my favourite memories..."
  • story-4.jpg     - "A moment I'll always keep..."
  • story-5.jpg     - "Us ❤️"
  • memory-1.jpg    - Polaroid #1 in the swipeable gallery
  • memory-2.jpg    - Polaroid #2 in the swipeable gallery
  • memory-3.jpg    - Polaroid #3 in the swipeable gallery
  • memory-4.jpg    - Polaroid #4 in the swipeable gallery
  • memory-5.jpg    - Polaroid #5 in the swipeable gallery
  • memory-6.jpg    - Polaroid #6 in the swipeable gallery
  • surprise.jpg    - Hidden photo revealed when he taps "One last thing..."
  • final.jpg       - Final romantic signoff photo

(You can use .jpg, .png, or .webp. If you have different filenames, 
 you can easily change them in config.js!)

--- OPTION 2: INSTANT PREVIEW IN THE BROWSER ---
Open index.html in your browser, tap the "Photos" button in the top right 
or tap any photo slot, and select pictures straight from your phone or laptop! 
They will show up instantly.
====================================================================
